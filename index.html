Four paths forward for AI

AI has crossed human-expert level in maths, science, cybersecurity and coding. The market has noticed: a cheap build-out in early 2025 is now priced for an exponential boom. The question is who pays for it.

Corporate power, wealth and compute are concentrating. That is fuelling a populist backlash in Europe and a collapse in how allies view the US. Meanwhile, defence, infrastructure and AI spending are pushing real rates up.

China has made itself self-sufficient while making the world depend on it. US tariffs diverted Chinese exports to Europe and Japan, so "mercantilism begets mercantilism" and the middle powers are next.

The fiscal limit isn't a debt/GDP number. It's the point where inflation, a weak currency or bubbles bite, and that depends on productivity, domestic savings and foreign demand for your assets.

$30T of AI market cap implies one future; the rest of the market implies another. The gap is the trade.
